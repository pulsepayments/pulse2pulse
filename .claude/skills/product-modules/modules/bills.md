# Bill pay

Pay Zambian bills from the app: ZESCO electricity tokens, airtime and data (MTN, Airtel, Zamtel), water, TV (DStv, GOtv) and school fees. A key diaspora use case is "pay my mum's electricity from London".

- Through a licensed local biller aggregator. Pulse shows billers, validates the customer reference, and shows the token or receipt.
- Cross-border flavour: a UK or US sender pays a Zambian bill directly. This reuses the Send leg and then the biller payout.
