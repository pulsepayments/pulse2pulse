# Virtual cards

USD virtual cards (physical later) for online spend (subscriptions, ads, SaaS), a major pain point for Zambian users.

- Requires a card issuer/processor plus a **BIN sponsor** (a licensed issuer). Pulse is program manager. Card T&Cs are with the issuer.
- Funding: from the user's **fiat** balance held by the partner (never Pulse). Stablecoin-linked cards (Rain, Bridge-Visa) are deferred behind a counsel gate.
- Features: create or freeze card, spend limits, merchant notifications, 3DS, disputes routed to the issuer, card-art branding.
- Risks: BIN sponsor appetite for African cardholders, chargeback and fraud exposure in the program agreement, FX and decline rates on international merchants.
