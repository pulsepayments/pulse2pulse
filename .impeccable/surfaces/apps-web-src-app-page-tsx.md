# Surface: Pulse marketing site + send-flow preview

Scope: `/` (Persuade: waitlist), `/preview` (Operate: interactive send-flow demo), `/privacy` (Read). Audience: investors and HNW individuals moving their own money UK/US → Zambia (£10k–£3M); prospective investors in Pulse and partners. Action: join the waitlist (role-tagged). Proof: no real customers or partners exist, so the proof is the demonstrated mechanism (a live illustrative quote and tracker), labelled as a preview. Constraints: compliance copy (no bank/deposit/guaranteed/crypto framing); speed stated as a design target with a footnote.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Money keeps a timetable. Pulse turns a weeks-long wire into a scheduled departure with an exact arrival time. It refuses the category default of a gradient hero, phone mockup and three feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: The Swiss/International typographic timetable. A strict 12-column grid; hairline rules; graphite (#16181A-ish) and paper-white grounds; one committed field colour, **Zambian copper**, owning whole regions (the quote board, the waitlist band). Grotesk type (Archivo, including its condensed widths) with tabular figures everywhere. Times, platforms and status columns. Components are timetable rows, column headers and status cells, not cards.
STORY: The visitor understands that Pulse moves serious money into Zambia on a timetable, believes it from the working quote and the arrival board, then joins the waitlist (or opens the preview).
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5 cols: the wordmark, the headline "Six figures to Lusaka. Same timetable as an email." (or similar) and one line. Right 7 cols: a copper **departure board** that is the working quote. You enter the amount, choose GBP/USD and the destination currency, and the rows update: Departs · Converts · Lands, with clock times and the amount received. "Join the waitlist" sits directly under the board. On mobile the board stacks under the headline, above the fold.
FORM: The Timetable, position 6 on my ordered list; seed: 858c2c631c786365. Raise from Copper (declined pick): total commitment to one metal colour as a field, not an accent. Signature interaction: the timetable re-times live as the amount changes (times tick in a split-flap-like roll, honouring reduced motion). Motion grammar: row-by-row settle, 180ms ease-out, never decorative.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
